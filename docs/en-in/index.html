      How to move through the hall

      All ZOLLER products are arranged in seven product worlds around the central plaza with the ZOLLER symbol.

      Mouse

      • Drag – rotate the hall
      • Right-click / ⇧ + drag – pan
      • Scroll / trackpad pinch – zoom towards the cursor
      • Double-click the floor – zoom in
      • Click a product – fly there & details
      • Click a sign – go to product world

      Keyboard

      • + − – zoom
      • ← → – previous / next product
      • 1–7 – product world
      • H – overview · T – tour
      • G – walk · / – search
      • Esc – close / back

      Walk

      • WASD – walk, QE – turn
      • Drag – look around
      • Click the floor – walk there
      • Touch: tap to walk, two fingers forward/back

      The yellow line on the floor marks the exhibition area – you cannot go beyond it. Every product links to its page on the ZOLLER website.

      Choose country and language

      The showroom and its product links switch to the ZOLLER website of the selected country.
